[b]Cruesoe's mod settings preset for the Genesis suite.[/b]

Applies preferred settings to third-party mods installed alongside Genesis, the same way ferny's Mod Configs does for the original Progression modpack.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Sets Genesis's tuning choices in the supported third-party mods it finds installed.
[*]Built fresh for Genesis rather than forked from ferny's Mod Configs.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save.
